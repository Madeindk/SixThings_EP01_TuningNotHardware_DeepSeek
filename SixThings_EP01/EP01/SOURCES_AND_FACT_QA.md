### Contradictions / nuances found and what changed (v2)
- Propwash cause (31 vs earlier script). Rosser shows the simple "own turbulence" story is incomplete: blades stall in reverse flow and suddenly regain thrust, and prop pitch is the biggest single lever. S03 now gives both explanations, says this sign "sits closest to the border", names lower-pitch props as far more forgiving, and frames dynamic idle as keeping props out of the stalled region. The sign stays in the episode because settings still matter (D-term, filter delay, dynamic idle), as 31, 34 and 35 also show.
- High-throttle oscillation (33). PX4 attributes part of it to thrust non-linearity and prefers a thrust curve over TPA. S08 now explains why hover-tuned gains can be too strong near full throttle (thrust stops growing in a straight line with motor command) and no longer implies TPA is the only tool.
- Hardware tell (42). Added to S09: a loose prop, rough bearing or failing motor often shows up as one motor driven harder than the other three; swap the suspect motor to another arm and see whether the problem follows it.
- No contradiction found for signs 2, 3, 4, 5 beyond source wording; those scenes changed only to remove source attribution.
